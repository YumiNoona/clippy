Open Loop ↗

If it isn’t running, double-click Open Loop in this folder, or use the Loop shortcut on your desktop.

How do I know it started?

Look beside the Windows clock (or click the ^ hidden-icons arrow). A green dot means Loop is running. A red dot means it is stopped. Hover over it to read the status. Double-click to open Loop; right-click for Start, Stop, or Help.

The green dot means the PC server is ready. Your phone must still be paired and on the same network. Windows may hide the icon or suppress its notification.

01 · CONNECT

Pair your phone

  1. Connect the PC and phone to the same Wi-Fi.
  2. On the PC, open Connect devices → Pair a phone or tablet.
  3. Scan the QR with the phone’s camera and open the link.

Make a fresh code for each device. Android, iPhone, and tablets use their browser.

02 · TRANSFER

Copy and use

  1. On Windows, copy normally. The Loop tray captures new text, links, images, and copied files automatically.
  2. On the other device, press Copy beside the item.
  3. On a phone or tablet, tap Paste to send a copy, or use New text / Add file.

Images and files must be under 2 MB; folders are skipped. Mobile browsers cannot watch copies in other apps in the background. Where a browser already grants clipboard read permission, Loop reads new text while its page is focused. Right-click the Windows tray and uncheck Automatically capture copies to pause capture.

Close the window. Keep the PC awake.

Loop starts quietly after Windows sign-in on this PC. Its window can stay closed. The PC must still be awake during transfers, including phone-to-tablet transfers. Keep Loop open on the phone while sending or receiving.

Keyboard shortcuts and quick clipboard

Ctrl+Alt+V opens the Windows quick clipboard panel. Click a clip or press Enter to copy it, then Ctrl+V to paste. Use Pin / Unpin, Ctrl+P, or swipe left / press Delete to remove a clip. Clear unpinned removes all unpinned clips from the shared library, including images and files; pinned clips stay. Escape or the close button hides the panel. Drag its heading to move it.

In the full app: Ctrl+K searches, Ctrl+Shift+L creates a text clip, Ctrl+Enter saves the editor, and ↑ / ↓ moves through clips. Ctrl+C copies the focused clip. Press ? to see the shortcuts. On Mac, use ⌘ for the in-app Ctrl shortcuts.

The Windows tray must be running for Ctrl+Alt+V. If another program uses that shortcut, use the tray menu. Images and files are available in the full app.

Shared pins, deletions, and device removal

Pinning or deleting a clip updates the same shared item on all connected devices. Open devices pull changes every few seconds. Offline devices apply changes when they reconnect; deleted clips stay deleted.

In the Devices sidebar, use the × beside another browser/device to remove it. On a phone, open the navigation menu first. Its saved clips remain in the shared library. That browser must use a fresh pairing code to sync again. Windows clipboard capture is managed from the tray menu.

At the office or on a hotspot

Connect your PC and phone to the same Wi-Fi. If office Wi-Fi blocks nearby devices, connect the PC to your phone’s hotspot instead. Generate a fresh QR after changing networks.

For another Windows PC, use app → Make office copy.cmd. It creates a ZIP in Downloads without your saved clips or access key. Unzip it on that PC, run Open Loop, then app → Enable Wi-Fi.cmd once. Windows may ask for administrator approval. That PC gets its own library.

If a phone won’t connect

Check that the PC is awake, both devices are on the same reachable network, and the QR is fresh. Codes work once and expire after five minutes.

Try app → Enable Wi-Fi.cmd, or use your own hotspot. An old bookmark may stop working when the PC’s Wi-Fi address changes.

Transfers use local HTTP, so use trusted home Wi-Fi or a secured hotspot. Sync any pending clips before changing networks.
